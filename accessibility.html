IESS wants clients, learners and visitors to be able to find information and complete essential tasks with confidence.

Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at Level AA for the public website. Accessibility is an ongoing requirement: content, forms, downloads and portal features are reviewed as services change.

Public website

The public site is designed with the following support:

  • Semantic page structure, headings and landmarks.
  • A skip link to the main content area.
  • Keyboard operation for navigation, menus and form controls.
  • Visible focus indicators.
  • Text alternatives for informative images and labels for form fields.
  • Responsive layouts that support zoom and narrower screens.
  • Reduced-motion behavior when the device requests it.

Portal access

The portal contains operational and assessment services. Some training assessments require timed, supervised or secure conditions for academic integrity. If a required assessment feature conflicts with an access need, contact IESS before the assessment so we can discuss a reasonable arrangement.

Documents

The official letterhead is available only inside the authenticated staff portal as a PDF for fixed-layout printing and as an editable Word file. For documents that must be accessible, use real headings, reading order, alternative text and descriptive link text in the body content. Do not place essential text inside the logo, seal or decorative images.

Feedback

If something on this website or in a downloadable document is difficult to use, tell us the page, device, assistive technology and task involved. We will consider the issue and, where possible, provide the information in another format.
