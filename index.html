Common questions

Before you reach out.

A few practical answers about our services, training and digital tools.

What types of projects does IESS handle?

We support residential, commercial and institutional energy needs, including solar and backup systems, monitoring, security, audits, technical proposals and installation support.

How do I request a solar quotation?

Use the contact form or call our team. A useful first conversation includes your location, the equipment you want to power, your preferred backup duration and whether you already have an inverter or battery system.

Who can use the IESS portal?

The portal supports authorised staff and registered training participants. Portal access is issued by IESS; contact the team if you need help with your credentials or have lost access.

How are IESS training certificates checked?

Each issued certificate carries a unique IESS certificate number and a verification route. Employers or institutions can confirm a certificate through the portal or by contacting IESS directly with the certificate number.

Can I use the IESS letterhead for my own documents?

The official letterhead is intended for authorised IESS correspondence. Staff and approved representatives may use the editable Word version for official letters, reports and proposals.
