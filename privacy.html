Legal & privacy

Privacy policy

This policy explains how Integrated Energy & Smart Systems Ltd handles information connected with this website and its online services.

Overview Information we receive How we use it Cookies and storage Sharing and service providers Retention and security Your choices Contact

Effective: 26 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 26 October 2026

Overview

IESS respects the privacy of clients, learners, portal users and website visitors. This website is primarily informational. It does not use advertising trackers, create visitor profiles or sell personal information.

The IESS portal is a separate service for authorised staff and registered participants. Portal use may involve account information, training records, attendance, assessment results and certificates. Access to that service should be limited to authorised users.

Information we receive

Information you choose to send

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, organisation, project details or other information in your message. You may also send information when requesting portal access, training information or a quotation.

Website and device information

Our hosting provider may process basic technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as request time, requested page, browser type and network information. We do not use this site to build advertising profiles.

Portal information

If you are authorised to use the portal, information may include your name, contact details, role, cohort participation, attendance, assessment activity, scores, certificate records and support or access logs.

How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested information or quotations.
  • Deliver training, assessments, attendance records and certification services.
  • Administer authorised portal access and maintain service security.
  • Prepare corporate documents and maintain appropriate business records.
  • Meet legal, regulatory or professional obligations.
  • Improve the usefulness, reliability and accessibility of our services.

Cookies and browser storage

This public website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which may receive standard request information from your browser when the page loads.

The IESS portal may use browser storage to preserve an active session and cache operational data. Portal users should use a trusted device and sign out when finished, particularly on shared computers. Browser or hosting providers may use essential security and delivery mechanisms outside IESS's direct control.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that support hosting, cloud storage, email delivery, communications or technical operations. We expect those providers to process information only for the relevant service.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect a person from harm, to investigate misuse, to protect legal rights or where you have authorised the disclosure.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including training records, certificates, business administration, security and legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of record and the relationship with IESS.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We use access controls, secure service configuration and limited disclosure principles where practicable. Users should report suspected unauthorised access promptly and should not share portal passwords, participant credentials or certificate details unnecessarily.

Your choices

Subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping needs, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, or object to a particular use. You may also ask us to stop non-essential communications.

Send a request using the contact details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

Contact form note: the form on this website prepares an email in your own email application. IESS does not receive the message until you choose to send that email.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to integratedpower.iess@gmail.com or raised by phone on +234 816 378 7297.

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of the page identifies the current version.
